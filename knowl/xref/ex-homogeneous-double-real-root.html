Example 3.2.14. Homogeneous ODE — Double Real Root.

Find the general solution of \(y''(t) - 2y'(t) + y(t) = 0\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution. The characteristic polynomial is
\begin{equation*} P(\lambda) = \lambda^2 - 2\lambda + 1 = (\lambda - 1)^2. \end{equation*}
The root \(\lambda_0 = 1\) has multiplicity \(2\) (Case 3a). The two fundamental solutions are
\begin{equation*} y_1(t) = e^t, \qquad y_2(t) = t\, e^t. \end{equation*}
The general solution is
\begin{equation*} y(t) = C_1\, e^t + C_2\, t\, e^t, \qquad C_1, C_2 \in \R. \end{equation*}
