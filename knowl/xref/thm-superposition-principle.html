Theorem 3.2.17. Superposition Principle.

Let \(y_p(t)\) be any particular solution of the inhomogeneous ODE
\begin{equation*} y^{(n)} + a_{n-1} y^{(n-1)} + \cdots + a_0 y = f(t). \end{equation*}
Let \(y_h(t) = C_1 y_1(t) + \cdots + C_n y_n(t)\) be the general solution of the corresponding homogeneous ODE (i.e.\ with \(f \equiv 0\)). Then the general solution of the inhomogeneous ODE is
\begin{equation*} y(t) = y_h(t) + y_p(t), \end{equation*}
and every solution of the inhomogeneous ODE has this form for some choice of \(C_1, \ldots, C_n \in \R\text{.}\)
