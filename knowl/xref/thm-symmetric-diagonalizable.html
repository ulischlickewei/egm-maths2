Theorem 1.3.27. Diagonalizability of Symmetric Matrices.

Let \(A\) be a symmetric \(n \times n\) matrix (i.e. \(A^\top = A\)). Then:
🔗
  • \(A\) is always diagonalizable.
    🔗
    🔗
  • There exist \(n\) pairwise orthogonal eigenvectors of \(A\text{.}\) In particular, \(A\) can be diagonalized by an orthogonal matrix \(P\text{,}\) meaning \(P^{-1} = P^\top\text{.}\)

    🔗
