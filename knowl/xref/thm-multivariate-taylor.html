Theorem 2.2.5. Multivariate Taylor Expansion of Order 2.

Let \(f \colon D \to \R\) be a function of \(n\) variables whose second-order partial derivatives exist and are continuous on an open set \(D \subseteq \R^n\text{.}\) Let \(\mathbf{x}_0, \mathbf{x} \in D\) be such that the line segment connecting them lies entirely in \(D\text{.}\) Then
\begin{equation*} f(\mathbf{x}) = f(\mathbf{x}_0) + \nabla f(\mathbf{x}_0) \cdot (\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{x}_0) + \tfrac{1}{2}(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}_0)^\top H_f(\mathbf{x}_0) (\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}_0) + R_2(\mathbf{x}), \end{equation*}
where the remainder satisfies
\begin{equation*} \lim_{\mathbf{x} \to \mathbf{x}_0} \frac{R_2(\mathbf{x})}{\|\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{x}_0\|^2} = 0. \end{equation*}
🔗
Explicitly, the three terms of the Taylor polynomial are:
  • Zeroth order: \(f(\mathbf{x}_0)\) — the function value at the expansion point.
    🔗
    🔗
  • First order: \(\nabla f(\mathbf{x}_0) \cdot (\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{x}_0)\) — the tangent plane correction (linear term).
    🔗
    🔗
  • Second order: \(\tfrac{1}{2}(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}_0)^\top H_f(\mathbf{x}_0)(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}_0)\) — the curvature correction (quadratic term).
